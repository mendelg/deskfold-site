Deskfold Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

In short: Deskfold does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything stays on your Mac.

What Deskfold collects

Nothing. Deskfold has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it makes no network connections.

What stays on your Mac

To show your folders, Deskfold saves a small settings file on your Mac. It contains your folder names, colours, the apps in each folder and your layout choices. This file lives inside Deskfold's own app container. It is never sent anywhere, and deleting Deskfold removes it.

Apps you add to folders

Deskfold reads the names and icons of apps installed on your Mac so it can show them in folders and launch them when you click. It only reads apps you choose or drag in, plus your Applications folder when building starter folders. It does not look inside your documents.

Open at login

If you turn on "Open Deskfold at login", Deskfold registers itself with macOS so it starts when you log in. You can turn this off at any time in Deskfold's Settings or in System Settings › General › Login Items.

Third parties

Deskfold includes no third-party code that collects data. If you buy Deskfold, the purchase is handled by Apple through the Mac App Store under Apple's own privacy policy; we receive no personal information about you.

Children

Deskfold collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
